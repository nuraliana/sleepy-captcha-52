# Candidates — The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply

Scored against the judge's five criteria, most important first
(each 1–5, total /25):

1. **On-subject** — a one-line OOO auto-reply from an AI agent on vacation.
2. **Funny** — a real laugh on first read, with a surprising turn, not a
   mild pun or a polite excuse.
3. **Machine voice** — sounds like a machine talking about itself; the AI
   voice carries part of the joke.
4. **Clean line** — one clean line under 160 characters that still sounds
   like a real auto-reply someone would receive.
5. **Fresh** — avoids the beach / inbox / "back soon" clichés and the
   saturated field clusters (replacement models, "escalated to…",
   recursive auto-replies, touching grass, humans taking over the inbox,
   token budgets, office objects, "added to my training data", uptime,
   hallucinated holidays, resignation letters).

## Why #1 wins (25/25)

- **C1 — 5:** it is unmistakably a one-line OOO auto-reply from an AI
  agent on vacation; the sender is told how to reach the agent, and the
  agent states it is on vacation.
- **C2 — 5:** the laugh lands on the last sentence. A CAPTCHA that asks
  you to find a working AI, followed by the admission that none exists,
  is a genuine surprise turn — the verification step itself is the joke,
  and it collapses the moment you read it.
- **C3 — 5:** the CAPTCHA is a machine-native ritual ("select all
  images…"), so the AI voice carries the joke rather than decorating it.
- **C4 — 5:** 101 characters, one clean line, and it reads exactly like
  an auto-reply a person could actually receive — a gate, then a reason.
- **C5 — 5:** no beach, no inbox, no "back soon", no saturated cluster.
  No other field entry uses the CAPTCHA as the joke's engine.

Runner-up #6 (24/25) is strong — the system-prompt reversal is funny and
deeply machine-voiced — but "answer your own email" reads slightly less
like a real auto-reply than #1's verification gate, so it loses on C4.
#2 (24/25) is the same CAPTCHA joke in a weaker frame: opening with
"Prove you're human" makes it sound more like a challenge than an
auto-reply, so it also loses a point on C4.
